# AMAkids source mirror and archive

This folder contains the original downloaded site mirror and the working page history. It is kept as a reference archive. The clean handoff package and current source of truth are now in [`../ready-for-develop`](../ready-for-develop/README.md). Start Live Server from `ready-for-develop/index.html`; make page and asset changes there. GitHub Pages also publishes `ready-for-develop`.

The mirror includes fetched media files. The franchise page is a locally redesigned prototype. The presentation CTA appears near the top of the page; the country-specific financial model CTA is beside the partnership proposal section. They use one modal whose copy changes by request type. The presentation is downloaded after the form, and the page also opens an email draft. In this local preview, no contact data is stored or sent automatically: the visitor must send the draft. For real lead capture on a published site, connect an approved form/CRM endpoint, then send or unlock the PDF after successful submission.

The product story is split into separate platform and motivation, CRM, early-results, advanced-skills and Olympiad sections. It mentions the mobile app, AR textbooks and the teaching package. The page embeds the platform overview, CRM walkthrough, Double Power, Caesar and International Mental Arithmetic Olympiad 2025 videos. Each video has a custom branded cover in `images/site/franchise/posters/covers/`, designed separately from its footage. The two supplied World Cup photos have been cropped to matching 16:9 image files in `images/site/franchise/events/`. Partner stories and a separate teacher-training video block are omitted until those materials are ready. The partnership section includes launch-cost and recurring-cost checklists without financial amounts; exact estimates are country- and model-specific.

The FAQ is grouped by learning, partnership and launch, territory and operations, and costs and agreements. It covers partner experience, child development, teaching materials, training, local responsibilities and the market-specific financial estimate.

See [FRANCHISE-PAGE-PLAN.md](FRANCHISE-PAGE-PLAN.md) for the current page structure, FAQ draft and partner interview checklist.
