# Investor financial section — recommended structure

## Slide 1 — One center. Clear economics.

Use `financial-model-one-center-investor-en.png` as a full-slide 16:9 image.

The slide intentionally shows only four numbers:

- initial investment;
- target-case payback;
- break-even student count;
- Year 2 EBITDA margin.

The 15-month payback is a target-performance case, not the base case. It assumes:

- four groups at launch, six groups by month 7 and eight groups by month 13;
- 85%+ seat occupancy;
- approximately 10 students per group;
- monthly churn at or below 3%;
- annual re-enrollment at or above 70%;
- customer acquisition cost at or below €45 per new paying student;
- average course price approximately 5% above the base case;
- education VAT exemption remains applicable;
- operating costs remain within the target ratios below.

## Slide 2 — What the initial investment covers

### Title

**A complete setup for launch**

### Five investment blocks

1. **Franchise and legal setup**  
   Franchise or territory fee, company registration, contracts and permits.

2. **Premises**  
   Rental deposit, fit-out, furniture, exterior and interior branding.

3. **Classroom equipment**  
   Laptops, screens, teaching equipment and initial learning materials.

4. **Pre-opening**  
   Launch marketing, recruitment, onboarding and teacher training.

5. **Working-capital reserve**  
   Liquidity for ramp-up, seasonality and unexpected operating costs.

Footer:

> Opening investment and working-capital reserve are presented separately: the reserve is liquidity, not an expense unless it is used.

## Slide 3 — What a successful center must achieve

### Title

**The operating targets behind the return**

| KPI | Target level |
|---|---:|
| Seat occupancy | 85–90%+ |
| Average group size | 9–10 students |
| Monthly student churn | ≤3% |
| Annual re-enrollment | ≥70% |
| Lead-to-paid conversion | 9–12% |
| Customer acquisition cost | ≤€45 |
| Rent and occupancy costs | ≤15% of net revenue |
| Total people cost | ≤30% of net revenue |
| Ongoing marketing | ≤10% of net revenue |
| Mature EBITDA margin | ≥20% |
| Cash reserve | 2–3 months of fixed costs |

Recommended closing statement:

> A center becomes highly profitable when groups are filled before fixed costs and headcount are expanded.

## Slide 4 — Scenario range

### Title

**Returns depend on execution**

| KPI | Downside | Base | Target performance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Payback | 46 months | 19 months | 15 months |
| Break-even | ~38 students | ~33 students | ~31 students |
| Year 2 revenue | ~€55K | ~€66K | ~€92K |
| Year 2 EBITDA margin | ~11% | ~28% | ~33% |
| Funding need incl. reserve | ~€23K | ~€21K | ~€21K |

Footer:

> The target case requires faster group expansion and stronger retention; it should never be presented as a guaranteed return.

## Short version

For a compact investor deck, use only three financial slides:

1. **One center. Clear economics.**
2. **What a successful center must achieve.**
3. **Scenario range.**

Move the detailed investment composition to the appendix or due-diligence section.
